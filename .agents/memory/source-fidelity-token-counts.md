---
name: Source fidelity and honest token counts
description: Original evidence outranks generated memory; distinguish actual provider usage from fallback budgeting estimates.
---

User corrections and original documents take priority over earlier assistant assertions and generated Tractatus summaries. Preserve original messages, provenance and contradictions; an explicit denial may establish an unknown role, not a guessed replacement.

**Why:** The user demanded repair after the app repeatedly confused judge/trustee roles and replaced invented dates with further inventions. Compression cannot make model output into verified evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve source access across compaction and fresh chats. Keep quoted model claims, user hypotheses and independently supplied documents distinct. Consistency warnings must not rewrite or withhold ordinary answers.

A generation provider's token-count endpoint can be unavailable even when its generation endpoint works. A byte-per-token bound can artificially discard a complete conversation that actually fits.

**Why:** This occurred during the repair: the conservative byte bound excluded messages, while the complete uploaded conversation subsequently fit and produced a source-grounded response.

**How to apply:** Label fallback tokenizer budgets as estimates, never actual counts or proven bounds. Actual counts come from provider responses, including cached input where reported. Retain exact model/version/parameters in owner-only traces; do not silently substitute models.

A historical document cannot disprove a later user-reported development merely because the document does not describe it.

**Why:** Even with the entire conversation present, a model treated explicitly completed procedural periods as unknown by privileging an older hearing transcript.

**How to apply:** Preserve sourced event-status updates separately from dates. Acknowledge the later report as a report, without claiming independent court verification or inventing an exact completion date.

Provider aliases may resolve to a different reported version than the literal requested model ID.

**Why:** Real provider responses exposed alias/version differences during verification; a successful request does not establish the provider's maximum context capacity.

**How to apply:** Expose both requested and reported models. Label conservative configured budgets as budgets, not verified physical capacities. Never interpret rate limits as permission to shorten history or change models.

A recalled citation is permission to inspect its original source only within the project where the citation was offered, not permission to follow a moved chat into a different project.

**Why:** A stale citation can otherwise make one project's evidence appear to come from another, even when the user owns both projects.

**How to apply:** Resolve source links against current ownership and current project membership. Show an unavailable-source message after moves or deletion; do not automatically navigate or fall back to cached originals.
